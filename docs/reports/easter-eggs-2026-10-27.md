# Easter egg regressions — 2026-10-27

- ICS: the PlatLogo window is transparent over the selected wallpaper. Live wallpapers remain visible there; Nyandroid keeps its own blue sky.
- Jelly Bean: live wallpaper remains visible behind both the logo and BeanBag. KitKat also keeps its wallpaper behind the initial K window.
- ICS: the touch release that launches Nyandroid is consumed, so its synthesized click cannot immediately close the new scene. Its 20 stars and 20 droids use the scene clock for their six 200 ms / twelve 80 ms bitmap frames. All eighteen images are decoded before playback. These essential content animations keep playing with reduced motion; the earlier static-star exception was incorrect and is removed.
- KitKat: a tap rotates K through a full turn in 700 ms, including reduced-motion mode. The K-to-logo reveal retains its fade, rotation and growth timings; reduced motion only removes overshoot. Tile placement moves and turns for 500 ms, including automatic shuffling after the native five-second delay. Reduced motion uses a plain decelerating movement without overshoot, keeping the game's essential motion.
- Gingerbread through Nougat: easter egg surfaces and their children suppress selection, native image dragging and WebKit image callouts. The interactive KitKat logo keeps its pointer target.

Validation: `node tests/browser/easter-eggs.mjs` passed all three affected releases at 1280/390 px with both motion preferences, using actual CDP touch holds/releases. It checks the actual rendered star and droid bitmap URLs over time plus distinct decoded RGBA pixels (not only translation, bookkeeping attributes or differently named copies), a real touch tap followed by intermediate and completed K rotations, the animated logo reveal, intermediate tile transforms, automatic shuffling, wallpaper visibility and context-menu suppression. Additional mobile selection/wallpaper checks cover 2.3.6, 5.1.1, 6.0.1 and 7.1.2. Screenshots were inspected. `npm test`: 275/275 passed.

Follow-up source check: the previous tests explicitly accepted frozen stars in reduced-motion mode and did not check droid body frames or the K tap. Those gaps are now covered in both modes and at both widths.
