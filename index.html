HOW IT WORKS

A simulator, not an emulator

An emulator boots the real Android system on virtual hardware. This site rebuilds every screen in HTML, CSS and JavaScript instead, taking the texts, icons, sounds and behaviour from each phone's original factory image.

  • Starts right awayNo multi-gigabyte system image to download and nothing to install: pick a version and the phone starts in your browser.
  • Light on your computerAn emulator needs a fast processor, hardware virtualization and several gigabytes of memory. This runs in a single browser tab, so an older laptop or even a phone is enough.
  • The Google apps tooThe servers behind the old Google apps are long gone, so even a real emulator would show them empty. Here they come back with demo data.

The trade-off: there is no real network, calling or app installation. Whatever would need them is still shown, but marked as not part of the simulator.
